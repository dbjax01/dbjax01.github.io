I work at the intersection of business and education.

Why financial literacy

Most students will make real money decisions before they ever take a business class: a first paycheck, a first loan, a first lease. I believe school should prepare them for those moments, and I have wanted to be a teacher for as long as I can remember.

Read my career goals

How I'd teach it

Pick a first money decision.

Students build a real budget.

They plan where a paycheck goes, with real prices and real trade-offs.

At Kanapaha, every table got a different budget, from $600 to $2,000, and had to build a complete, compatible computer.

See the classroom

Students compare real loan offers.

They line offers up side by side and learn what borrowing actually costs.

My graduate coursework covered real estate finance and corporate finance, so I can explain how lending works, not just define it.

Students make sense of housing.

For most families, housing is the biggest financial decision. Students learn how renting, buying, and credit fit together.
