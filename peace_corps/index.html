Peace Corps: Community Economic Development

Since April 2026 I have lived and worked in Cajamarca, Peru, as a Community Economic Development Volunteer. I work and teach entirely in Spanish, and each part of my service shapes how I will teach back home.

Where I serve

I live and work in the Cajamarca Region of northern Peru, where I do my work in Spanish. Hover over or tap the map to explore Peru's regions.

Financial education

I teach practical money skills to community members, in Spanish and with examples from their own lives, so they can use what they learn right away. My course has twelve weekly sessions.

Women's economic empowerment

I lead several women's groups that combine personal development with practical business skills. I partner with social programs such as Cuna Más, an early childhood program, and Juntos, a program that supports families in poverty. Each class has 18 participants and meets weekly.

When I learned that some participants could not read, I rebuilt my lessons to be more visual, collaborative, and hands-on. Those changes pushed me to be creative, and the lessons turned out to be more engaging for everyone.
