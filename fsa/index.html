Florida Scholars Academy at Ocala Youth Academy

From December 2024 to February 2026 I taught math and science on site at a residential juvenile justice facility, through Florida Virtual School's partnership with the Florida Department of Juvenile Justice.

What the work involved

  • Combined the FLVS online platform with in-person teaching so each student had an individualized, standards-based path.
  • Adapted lessons for different learning styles in a nontraditional, secure setting.
  • Worked with facility staff and FLVS administrators on state-standards alignment and student support services.
  • Supported academic growth and engagement for justice-involved youth, with a focus on long-term educational success.
