Career goals

I want to teach financial literacy in secondary education.

For as long as I can remember, I have wanted to be a teacher. In high school, a business ownership class pushed me toward entrepreneurship and financial literacy, and I went on to study both business and education at the University of Florida.

After graduating I looked for classroom roles in Alachua County that fit business and financial literacy, which led me to Kanapaha Middle School. Since then I've taught in a juvenile justice program and now serve in the Peace Corps, teaching financial education in Peru.

Next, I want a secondary classroom where students learn to budget, save, borrow wisely, and start something of their own.
